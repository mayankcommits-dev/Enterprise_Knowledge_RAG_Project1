Title: Queue Transaction Failure and Recovery Procedure

Purpose:
This procedure defines how operations teams should handle repeatedly failing automation queue transactions.

Procedure:
First determine whether the failure is a business exception or a system exception. Business exceptions caused by invalid input data should not be automatically retried unless the underlying data has been corrected.

System exceptions caused by temporary infrastructure or service failures may be retried according to the configured retry policy.

Before retrying a transaction, review the error message, execution logs, previous retry attempts, and dependent service status.

Transactions that continue failing after the permitted retry attempts should be moved for manual investigation rather than repeatedly reprocessed.

Operators must record the transaction identifier, failure category, retry count, and final resolution for troubleshooting and audit purposes.
