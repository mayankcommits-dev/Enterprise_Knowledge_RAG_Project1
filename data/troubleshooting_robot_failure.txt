Title: Troubleshooting Unattended Robot Execution Failures

Symptoms:
An unattended automation may fail to start, disconnect during execution, or remain unavailable even though a job has been scheduled.

Checks:
Verify that the runtime machine is online and accessible. Confirm that the robot or machine configuration is correctly connected to the automation platform.

Review job logs for authentication, application, selector, network, and dependency failures. Verify that required credentials and configuration assets are available to the process.

If the automation interacts with a user interface, confirm that the target application's state and selectors still match the version expected by the workflow.

Recovery:
Resolve the identified dependency or configuration problem before restarting the job. Do not repeatedly restart a failing automation without investigating the underlying error.

Escalation:
If the failure cannot be resolved from execution logs and standard checks, preserve the relevant logs, job identifier, machine information, and error details before escalation.
