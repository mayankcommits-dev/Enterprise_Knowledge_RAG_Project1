Incident ID: INC-2026-002
System: Invoice Automation
Category: OCR Processing
Severity: Medium
Date: 22 August 2026

Title: Invoice Processing Delays During OCR Service Degradation

Summary:
The invoice automation workflow experienced significant processing delays during a high-volume batch. Several invoice transactions exceeded the configured processing timeout.

Symptoms:
Invoice jobs remained in processing state longer than expected. Some transactions failed with timeout errors while waiting for OCR results.

Investigation:
The automation platform and queue infrastructure were operating normally. Monitoring showed increased response times from the external OCR service during the incident window.

Root Cause:
The external OCR service experienced degraded performance under high request volume. The automation workflow used a short fixed timeout and did not retry temporary OCR failures.

Resolution:
The OCR request timeout was increased and exponential-backoff retry logic was introduced for transient OCR failures.

Prevention:
OCR response latency should be monitored. Large invoice batches should use controlled request rates, and transient service failures should follow the approved retry strategy.
